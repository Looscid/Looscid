# Looscid terminology

Looscid has its own words. I use them everywhere: in the app, in code comments, in docs and in every label VoiceOver reads. This page lists them, plus the few rules that go with them.

## Words Looscid uses

- **Dream / Dreams:** something you publish on Looscid. Never "post", "toot" or "tweet".
- **Publish / published / "just published":** what you do when you send a Dream out. Never "posted".
- **Redream:** sharing someone else's Dream to your followers as it is.
- **Quote:** a Redream with your own words added on top.
- **Replies:** answers to a Dream, shown in its thread. Never "comments".
- **Liked:** the Dreams you liked, and the label for a Dream you've liked.
- **Dreamor / Dreamors:** the people on Looscid.
- **New Dreamor:** someone who just joined Looscid.
- **LooscidID:** your account and identity on Looscid.
- **Mutuals:** people who follow you and who you follow back. Never "Friends".
- **Circles:** small communities inside Looscid, each with its own feed, rules and mods. Written c/name, like c/music.
- **@name:** how people are written, like @someone. Circles get c/, people get @.
- **Cherry:** the AI built into Looscid.
- **Automated:** the label for bot accounts.

## The "post" rule

- The words "post", "posted" and "posting" never appear in the app.
- The only exceptions:
  - where a license or legal text requires them;
  - where another project's own name or wording requires them, for example a page describing another app.
- Hidden settings-search keywords may include "post", so someone who types it still finds the right setting. They're never shown or spoken, so they don't count.
- Code identifiers and technical terms like HTTP POST aren't copy, so they're fine too.

## Page titles

- Every page title follows "<Name> - <SLOGAN>".
- Examples: "Looscid - Accessibility first, always" and "Cherry - DO IT YOUR WAY".

## About headings

- Never write "About this account".
- Write "About <display name>", like "About Sam".
- If there's no display name, fall back to "About this LooscidID".
