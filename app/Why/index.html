Why Looscid?

Looscid is for everyone. Why I'm building it, and what makes it different.

Looscid is for everyone. No exceptions.

Most social apps are built for one kind of person, then patched for everyone else, if they ever get to it. I'm building Looscid the other way around: everyone first, from the first line of code.

I'm not building this to be nice. I'm building it because I care, and caring means doing the work.

Made for every body and every brain

Blind or low vision. Deaf or hard of hearing. A motor disability. A cognitive or learning disability. A seizure or vestibular condition. Looscid is for you. Vision doesn't matter. Looscid is for everyone.

Settings, Accessibility has a section for each:

  • Vision settings: text size, high contrast, bold text and spacing.
  • Hearing settings: captions and visual cues for sounds.
  • Motor settings: larger buttons and a strong focus outline.
  • Motion settings: Calm mode, Flash safety and Reduce Motion.
  • Commandbar settings: how Commandbar output is laid out and read.

I build Looscid to meet accessibility standards, and I want it tested by real people with disabilities.

Looscid aims to follow WCAG 2.2 and the spirit of the ADA. It hasn't been audited yet. If something doesn't work for you, tell me and I'll fix it.

Every screen reader, not just one

Most social apps get tested with one screen reader, if they get tested at all. I build Looscid for every screen reader: VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, Orca, and everything in between, plus braille displays. Every screen has one heading level 1, so you can jump straight to the top of any page.

Safe from flashing lights and motion

Most social apps play whatever shows up. If flashing lights or animations can hurt you, you still belong here. Flash safety is on by default, and nothing in Looscid flashes more than three times a second. Calm mode quiets things down. Zero animation mode is coming: one switch that stops all motion, autoplay and transitions.

Open source, for real

Most social apps keep their code to themselves. Looscid is open source under the GNU AGPL-3.0, so anyone can read the code, check what it does and help build it.

Moderation you can see

Most social apps moderate behind closed doors. Looscid's mod log is public: every Dream the mods remove is listed with the date and the reason, and a Dream can't be removed without one. Your reports stay private. In the Report Center, only you and the Looscid mods see your reports, and My reports shows what happened to each one.

Likes and dislikes, your way

Like what you like. Dislike what you don't. Your dislikes are private: only you ever see them, and Looscid never shows a dislike count. The Liked tab keeps both in one place.

Dreams and Circles

A Dream is what you share on Looscid. Circles are groups of Dreamors around one thing you like.

No ads

Looscid has no ads and doesn't sell your data.

Built with the people it protects

The people who live with these conditions should help build this. Their feedback matters most, and I want them at the table. If that's you, read how to help build Looscid or send feedback.

My full commitment is at the top of the accessibility guide.

Why I'm building this — Carter

I'm Carter, and I'm blind. I'm building Looscid because I believe apps should be for everyone.

If you have a problem, you should be heard. If you get banned, you should be heard. That's why Looscid has a public mod log and a Report Center where you get a real reply.

YOU MATTER. PEOPLE MATTER.

Help me build it

I don't want to guess what you need. I want people with all kinds of disabilities helping build Looscid: blind and low vision, Deaf and hard of hearing, motor, cognitive and learning, seizure and vestibular, and more. Code, test, or just tell me what's wrong. Looscid is free and open source.
