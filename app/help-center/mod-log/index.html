Mod log

Every Dream a Circle's mods or a Looscid admin removed is listed in public, with the date and the reason.

Steps

  1. When a Dream is removed, one line takes its place that everyone can read, like “Removed by the mods of Night Owls. Reason: Spam” or “Removed by a Looscid admin. Reason: Spam”.
  2. It always says who removed it, a Circle's mods or a Looscid admin, and why. A Dream can't be removed without a reason.
  3. The mod log below lists every removal: the date, which Dream (never its words), moderator or admin, and the reason.
  4. Each Circle has its own mod log too. Open the Circle, go to About, and press Mod log.
  5. Deleting your own Dream is different. If nobody replied, it's just gone. If it has replies, a line saying “Dream deleted” stays so the thread still makes sense, with no name and no words.
