Like, Redream and Quote

Show you like a Dream, or share it with your followers.

Steps

  1. Under every Dream are Replies, Redreams, Likes and More.
  2. Press Likes to like it. Press it again to undo.
  3. Press Redreams. Choose Redream to share it with your followers as it is, or Quote Dream to add your own words on top.
  4. Your likes are on your Profile, in the Liked tab.
