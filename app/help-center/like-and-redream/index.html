Like, Redream, Quote and the More button

Like a Dream, share it with your followers, and find everything else you can do in the More button on Dreams and profiles.

Under every Dream are Replies, Redreams, Likes and More. Here's what the last three do.

Likes

Press Likes to like a Dream. Press it again to undo, in case your thumb got excited. Every Dream you like is on your Profile, in the Liked tab.

Redreams and Quotes

Press Redreams. Pick Redream to share the Dream with your followers just as it is. Pick Quote Dream to add your own words on top, like “this is literally me”.

Your likes and Redreams sync through Rem with your key, so they're the same on all your devices. Only the counts are public. Nobody gets a list of every Dream you liked at 3 AM.

The More button on a Dream

Press More under a Dream and Dream Options opens. It has things like Copy link, and Pin to profile for your own Dreams. On your own Dream or reply, it also has Edit and Delete.

Deleting a Dream deletes it on Rem too. If nobody replied, it's just gone, like it never happened. If it has replies, a line saying “Dream deleted” stays, so the thread still makes sense. That line has no name and none of your words.

The More button on a profile

On someone's profile, press More. It has Share profile, Copy link, Add to list, Mute and Block. Use them whenever you need to. Your feed, your rules.

Changed your mind? Press Close, or Escape on a keyboard, to close a menu without choosing anything.
