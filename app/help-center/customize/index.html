Make Looscid fit you

Change how Looscid looks, sounds and moves.

Steps

  1. Press Open Appearance (Profile, More, Settings, Customization) for colors, text and the heading character.
  2. Press Open Earcons (Profile, More, Settings) for Looscid's short sounds and their volume.
  3. Press Open Accessibility (Profile, More, Settings) for vision, hearing, motion and more. Calm mode is on by default.
  4. Press Open Keyboard (Profile, More, Settings, General) for keys and your own shortcuts.
