Meet Cherry

Cherry is Looscid's assistant. It runs on your device, so your words aren't sent to an AI company.

Steps

  1. Turn Cherry on in Open Cherry (Profile, More, Settings).
  2. The first time, Cherry asks to download its model once. After that it works offline.
  3. Press Open Cherry (Profile, More), or press Create, then New Chat with Cherry.
  4. Type a question and send it. New Chat starts fresh, and Your History keeps your past chats.
  5. Each chat has Chat Options: Pin, Edit name and Delete.

Good to know

  • Turning Cherry off hides it everywhere.
