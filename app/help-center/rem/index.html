Rem: your Dreams on every device

Rem is Looscid's own server, the home of your public Dreams. Everything about it is on one screen, in LooscidID.

Steps

  1. See if Rem is online and which mode you're in. Local means your Dreams live on this device; Rem means your public Dreams sync to Rem.
  2. Put your Dreams on Rem. Choose All my Dreams, Let me pick, or Not now. Nothing older uploads until you choose.
  3. Your likes, ReDreams, pins, mutes and the threads you follow sync through Rem with your key, so they're the same on all your devices.
  4. Download or delete your Dreams on Rem. Delete my Rem data asks first, and Dreams on this device stay.
  5. Share your public Dreams to other networks too, with Bridges.

Good to know

  • I'm working on Sync to my other devices and Just this device switches for your settings. They aren't here yet.
