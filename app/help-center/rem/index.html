About Rem: where your Dreams live

Rem is Looscid's own server. It's where cooked Dreams live, so your Dreams show up on all your devices and other Dreamors can find them. Here's everything it does, everything it doesn't, and what it might have to do with sleep.

Okay, so. Rem. You've probably seen the name around Looscid and thought, “who is Rem and why does it have my Dreams?” Great question. Pull up a chair. This is the long version, with jokes, because servers are boring and I refuse to let them be.

So what even is Rem?

Rem is Looscid's protocol and its own server. It's the home of cooked Dreams. When you finish a Dream, press Dream, and its Audience is Everyone, it goes on Rem. That's how your Dreams show up on all your devices, and how other Dreamors find them in the feed. Without Rem, your Dreams would just sit on your phone like a diary with a lock on it. Which is fine for a diary. Less fine for a social app.

Rem runs on Cloudflare, it's open source, and you can download your Dreams from it anytime. Nobody's holding your Dreams hostage. If Rem were a bank, it would be the kind that lets you take all your money out in pennies without asking why.

Wait, why is it called Rem?

Fun fact: REM is also the part of sleep when you dream. Looscid is full of Dreams. Is that a coincidence? I'll let you decide. (It's a good name either way. Way better than Server Number 2.)

How Rem knows a Dream is really yours

Every Dream that goes on Rem is signed with your LooscidID key. Think of it like a signature nobody can copy, not even someone with really good handwriting. Rem checks that signature before it saves anything. So nobody can fake a Dream and pretend it's from you. If someone tries, Rem just says “nope” and moves on.

Rem keeps your public key and when it first saw it. It never gets your secret key, your email or your phone number. It doesn't even know your birthday, which is honestly kind of rude, but also good for privacy.

What goes on Rem

Cooked Dreams and replies set to Everyone. That's it for Dreams. A reply set to Everyone goes on Rem right under the Dream it answers, so threads stay together.

Your likes, Redreams, pins, mutes and the threads you follow also sync through Rem with your key, so they're the same on all your devices. Only like and Redream counts are public. Nobody can see a list of what you liked, so your secret love for 47 Dreams about frogs stays secret.

What stays on your device

Your Uncooked Dreams (drafts) stay on your device. They never go to Rem or anywhere else. Your half-finished Dream about how pineapple on pizza is actually amazing is safe from the internet until you decide to cook it.

Dreams set to Followers only, My Circles or Only this device stay on your device too. Your profile, settings and Soundpacks are saved on your device as well.

And this part matters: sharing is up to each Dream. If you turn sharing off on one Dream, that's the only one it changes. It doesn't matter when you turned it off, before Rem, after Rem, or in the middle of the night. If it's off, it's off. Your other Dreams don't change. Each Dream minds its own business.

What about my old Dreams?

If you made Dreams before Rem existed, or back when Looscid had a Local mode, I didn't forget about them. The next time you open Looscid, the ones set to Everyone go on Rem by themselves. It happens once. You don't have to press anything. Each one keeps its first date and is signed with your key, so your old Dreams show up looking exactly like they did, just more famous.

When it's done, Looscid tells you: “Your Dreams were synced to Rem.” Under it is a Learn more about Rem button with the short version of this page, plus a link to the Rem screen. It doesn't grab your focus or interrupt what you're doing. It just lets you know, politely, like a good roommate.

No key? Locked key?

If your LooscidID doesn't have a key yet, your cooked Dreams wait on your device. They go on Rem as soon as you make one. If your key is locked with a passcode, unlock it and your Dreams can sync again. Rem is patient. It's been waiting this whole time. It's fine. Totally fine.

Getting your Dreams off Rem

Want one Dream off Rem? Delete it, and it's deleted on Rem too. Done.

Want everything off Rem? Delete my Rem data deletes everything Rem has for your key: your Dreams, your lists, and your reports and votes. It asks first, because it's a big deal. Dreams on this device stay on this device, and Looscid won't put them back on Rem later. When you say delete, I mean delete.

Rather keep a copy first? Download my Dreams, with your other backups. Smart move.

The Rem screen

Open the Rem screen to see if Rem is online, sync your Dreams and lists right now, and read the About Rem part again whenever you want. You can come back as many times as you like. Rem won't get tired of you.

Sharing to other networks

Want your public Dreams on other networks too? Turn on Bridges. If you turn on Also share my Dreams to Nostr, a Dream set to Everyone goes to your Nostr relays too. Just know that copies on Nostr can't be fully deleted, because Nostr is like the internet's elephant. It never forgets.

What's coming

I'm working on Sync to my other devices and Just this device switches for your settings. They aren't here yet, so for now everything works the way this page says. When they're ready, this page gets an update, and probably another joke.

That's Rem. It holds your cooked Dreams, it checks every signature, it lets you leave whenever you want, and it never asks for your email. Honestly, it's a better friend than some people I know.

— Carter
