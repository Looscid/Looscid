Looscid Basics

Learn Looscid from the start, one short lesson at a time. Your progress is saved only on this device.

9 lessons

Start Lesson 1

Lessons

  1. Lesson 1: What is Looscid? Done

    Looscid is a free, open source social app that runs in your web browser, on any phone, tablet or computer.

  2. Lesson 2: Make your LooscidID Done

    Your LooscidID is your account. Looscid makes it on your device the first time you open it.

  3. Lesson 3: Get around Looscid Done

    Where everything is: the tab bar, the top bar, the More menu and the footer.

  4. Lesson 4: Write your first Dream Done

    A Dream can be a few words, a photo, a poll or a link.

  5. Lesson 5: Reply to a Dream Done

    Replies keep a conversation together in one thread.

  6. Lesson 6: Like, Redream and Quote Done

    Show you like a Dream, or share it with your followers.

  7. Lesson 7: Start a private chat Done

    Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

  8. Lesson 8: Find any setting Done

    Settings has every choice in Looscid, and a Search box to find one fast.

  9. Lesson 9: Back up and bring back your settings Done

    Save every setting as one file, and bring it back on any device.

Your progress is saved only on this device, in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
