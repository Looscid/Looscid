Looscid Basics

Learn Looscid from the start, one short lesson at a time. Your progress is saved only on this device.

9 lessons

Start Lesson 1

Lessons

  1. Lesson 1: What is Looscid? Done

    Looscid is a free, open source social app that runs in your web browser, on any phone, tablet or computer. No app store, no download, no money.

  2. Lesson 2: Make your LooscidID Done

    Your LooscidID is your account. Looscid makes it on your device the first time you open it, so there's no sign-up form and no email.

  3. Lesson 3: Find your way around Looscid Done

    Where everything is: the tab bar, the top bar, the More menu and the footer.

  4. Lesson 4: Write and cook a Dream Done

    A Dream can be a few words, a photo, a poll or a link. Here's how to write one, cook it, and send it out into the world.

  5. Lesson 5: Reply to Dreams and follow threads Done

    Replies keep a conversation together in one thread. Follow a thread to get an alert every time someone replies.

  6. Lesson 6: Like, Redream, Quote and the More button Done

    Like a Dream, share it with your followers, and find everything else you can do in the More button on Dreams and profiles.

  7. Lesson 7: Send a private chat Done

    Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

  8. Lesson 8: Find settings and make Looscid yours Done

    Settings has every choice in Looscid, with a Search box to find one fast. Change how Looscid looks, sounds and moves.

  9. Lesson 9: Back up, download or delete your data Done

    Save your settings as one file, back up everything with your key, download your Dreams, and see what's stored on this device.

Your progress is saved only on this device, in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
