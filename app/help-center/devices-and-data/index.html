Your devices and your data

See the devices using your LooscidID and everything Looscid keeps on this device.

Steps

  1. Press Open LooscidID (Profile, More, Settings).
  2. Press Devices to see this device and any others.
  3. Press Data to see what is stored on this device. You can export it or delete it there.
  4. Security checkup suggests ways to keep your LooscidID safe.
