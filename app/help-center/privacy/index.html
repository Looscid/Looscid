Privacy and who can reach you

Your Dreams, settings and chats stay on your device until you choose to send or share them.

Steps

  1. Press Open Privacy (Profile, More, Settings).
  2. Private account and Let others share my Dreams are at the top. Press one to turn it on or off.
  3. Permissions chooses who can see and reach you. Blocked and muted lists who you blocked or muted.
  4. Choose who can message you in Open Chats (Profile, More, Settings).
