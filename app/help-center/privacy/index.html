Privacy and security

Choose who can see and reach you, and keep your LooscidID safe. Dreams set to Everyone are public on Rem; your drafts and settings stay on your device.

Here's the deal: Dreams you cook to Everyone are public. They live on Rem, and anyone can read them. Your Uncooked Dreams, your settings, and Dreams set to Followers only, My Circles or Only this device stay on your device. Everything else on this page is about choosing who gets to see and reach you.

Choose who can see and reach you. Privacy comes first, in 3 parts: My profile, Chats, and Cherry and my stuff. Security is below them.

Your profile

Private account and Let others share my Dreams are at the top, in My profile. Press one to turn it on or off. Choose who can see, reply to and find you. Choose what's on your profile, like your Circles and the threads you follow.

People

Choose who can message you. See who and what you blocked or muted. That list can get long. No judgment.

Cherry

Choose what Cherry can see. Those are the same switches as on Cherry's own screen.

Keeping your key safe

Your key is basically the master key to your LooscidID, so treat it like your house key, not like a free sample. Lock your Nostr key with a passcode, so nothing else on this site can copy it. Back up everything and your key in one locked file, with Backup file. Then check how safe your LooscidID is. Security checkup tells you what to fix, nicely.
