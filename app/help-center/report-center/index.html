Report a Dream, a person or a Circle

Report a Dream, a person, a Circle or child safety, then follow what happens and talk to the mods. Reports are private.

Most Dreamors are great. Some are not. When something's wrong, the Report Center is where you tell the Looscid mods. Everything about reports is private.

Making a report

Press Report on a Dream (in its More button) or on someone's profile (in More). The Report Center opens with everything filled in. You can also open the Report Center yourself.

Pick what you're reporting: a Dream, a person, a Circle, a bug or an idea, or Child safety. Bugs and ideas go to Report a bug or share an idea instead. Then pick what's wrong: Spam, Mean or bullying, Unsafe or harmful, Child safety, or Something else. Add more info if you want, then press Send report.

Looscid says “ur report's submitted. check it in the Report Center anytime”. The person or Circle you reported never finds out who reported them. You're a secret agent now.

What happens next

My reports lists every report you made, with its status: sent, the mods are looking at it, done, or closed. Your bug reports are there too.

Each report is its own thread. Press Thread to read it, add more info for the mods, and see their answers. They reply as Looscid mods. When the status changes or the mods reply, you get an alert in Inbox, under Alerts.

Good to know

Only you and the Looscid mods can read your reports, and reading them needs your Looscid key. Delete my Rem data also deletes your reports and their threads.

If a child is in danger right now, call your local emergency number first. Looscid is fast, but not that fast.
