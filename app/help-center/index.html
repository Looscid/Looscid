    Popular articles

    1. Make your LooscidID

      Your LooscidID is your account. It's a secret key Looscid makes on your device in one step, so there's no sign-up form and no email.

    2. Write and cook a Dream

      A Dream can be a few words, a photo, a poll or a link. Here's how to write one, cook it, and send it out into the world.

    3. About Rem: where your Dreams live

      Rem is Looscid's own server. It's where cooked Dreams live, so your Dreams show up on all your devices and other Dreamors can find them. Here's everything it does, everything it doesn't, and what it might have to do with sleep.

    4. Send a private chat

      Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

    5. Find settings and make Looscid yours

      Settings has every choice in Looscid, with a Search box to find one fast. Change how Looscid looks, sounds and moves.

    6. Back up, download or delete your data

      Save your settings as one file, back up everything with your key, download your Dreams, and see what's stored on this device.
