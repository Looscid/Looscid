    Popular articles

    1. Make your LooscidID

      Your LooscidID is your account. Looscid makes it on your device the first time you open it.

    2. Write your first Dream

      A Dream can be a few words, a photo, a poll or a link.

    3. Start a private chat

      Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

    4. Find any setting

      Settings has every choice in Looscid, and a Search box to find one fast.

    5. Back up and bring back your settings

      Save every setting as one file, and bring it back on any device.

    6. Privacy and who can reach you

      Your Dreams, settings and chats stay on your device until you choose to send or share them.
