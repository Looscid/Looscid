Find your way around Looscid

Where everything is: the tab bar, the top bar, the More menu and the footer.

Looscid isn't a maze, but every app feels like one for the first five minutes. Here's the map.

The tab bar

The tab bar is at the bottom of the screen. It has Home, Discover, Create, Inbox and Profile. Press one to go there. That's the whole trick.

The top bar

The top bar has the Looscid logo and Create, on every screen. So if a Dream idea hits you in the middle of Settings, you don't have to go anywhere. Just press Create.

The More menu

Press Profile, then More, for everything else: Lists, Drafts, Cherry, Circles, Settings, Commandbar, Help, Feedback and Logout. Think of it as the junk drawer, except everything in it is actually useful.

Going back and closing things

Most screens have Back at the top left, and pop-ups have Close. On a keyboard, Escape closes things too.

The footer

The footer at the end of every screen has About Looscid, the Changelog, Terms, Privacy, and links to Looscid on GitHub, X, YouTube and Discord. Every screen also has its own address, so you can share it or bookmark it.

Keyboard people, this one's for you

Tab and Shift Tab move around, Enter and Space press, and Escape closes. Control K or Command K opens Commandbar from anywhere, which is honestly the fastest way to do anything. Type commands with Commandbar shows you how.

  • Every screen has one heading level 1, so the headings list (VoiceOver rotor, TalkBack headings, NVDA or JAWS H key, Narrator H key) takes you straight to the top of the screen.
  • Looscid speaks results once, politely, and only moves focus when you do something.
