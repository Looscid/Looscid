Get around Looscid

Where everything is: the tab bar, the top bar, the More menu and the footer.

Steps

  1. The tab bar at the bottom has Home, Discover, Create, Inbox and Profile. Press one to go there.
  2. The top bar has the Looscid logo and Create, on every screen.
  3. Press Profile, then More, for everything else: Lists, Drafts, Cherry, Circles, Settings, Commandbar, Help, Feedback and Logout.
  4. Most screens have Back at the top left, and pop-ups have Close.
  5. The footer at the end of every screen has About Looscid, the Changelog, Terms, Privacy and links to Looscid on GitHub, X, YouTube and Discord.
  6. Every screen has its own address, so you can share or bookmark it.

Good to know

  • On a keyboard: Tab and Shift Tab move, Enter and Space press, and Escape closes. Control K or Command K opens Commandbar from anywhere. See Use Commandbar.

  • Every screen has one heading level 1, so the headings list (VoiceOver rotor, TalkBack headings, NVDA or JAWS H key, Narrator H key) takes you straight to the top of the screen.
  • Looscid speaks results once, politely, and only moves focus when you do something.
