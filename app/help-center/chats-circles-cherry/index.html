Chats, Circles and Cherry

Private chats, Circles for groups of Dreamors, Cherry the built-in assistant, and your Inbox.

Articles in this collection

  1. Start a private chat

    Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

  2. Join or make a Circle

    A Circle is a group of Dreamors around one thing you like.

  3. Meet Cherry

    Cherry is Looscid's assistant. It runs on your device, so your words aren't sent to an AI company.

  4. Your Inbox and alerts

    Inbox has your alerts, like replies, mentions and likes, and your private chats.
