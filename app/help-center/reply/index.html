Reply to Dreams and follow threads

Replies keep a conversation together in one thread. Follow a thread to get an alert every time someone replies.

Under every Dream are four buttons: Replies, Redreams, Likes and More. Press Replies and the thread opens, with the Dream on top and every reply under it.

To join in, press Reply, type what you want to say, then press Reply again to send it. A reply set to Everyone goes on Rem, right under the Dream it answers, so the conversation stays in one piece.

Each reply has its own Thread Options button. It opens Reply Options, with things like Copy link, so you can send someone straight to the good part.

Following a thread

Found a thread that's too good to miss? Follow it. While the thread is open, press Follow Thread. It's just above the tab bar. Now every new reply shows up in your Inbox, under Followed Threads. It's like subscribing to drama, but for good stuff.

Your Profile has a Followed Threads tab with every thread you follow. When you've had enough, press Unfollow Thread in the same place. No hard feelings.

Choose who can see the threads you follow, in case your thread taste is a secret.
