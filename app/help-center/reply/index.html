Reply to a Dream

Replies keep a conversation together in one thread.

Steps

  1. Find the Dream. Under it are Replies, Redreams, Likes and More.
  2. Press Replies. The thread opens: the Dream and every reply to it.
  3. Press Reply, type what you want to say, then press Reply again to send it.
  4. Each reply has its own Thread Options button. It opens Reply Options, with things like Copy link.
