Dream card

one Dream in a feed: who made it, the words, and the Reply, Redream, Like and More buttons.

what it is

every Dream in Home, Discover, Circles and profiles is one of these. name and time up top, the Dream in the middle, then a row of buttons: Replies, Redreams, Likes and More.

the whole card is one article, so VoiceOver reads the Dream in one swipe, then the buttons one by one.

states

live demos, drawn w looscid's real styles. look-only: nothing in them does anything.

normal

a fresh Dream, nothing liked yet.

liked and Redreamed

the boxes are ticked when u liked and Redreamed it.

Redreamed by someone

a line on top says who Redreamed it into ur feed.

verified and edited

"Verified" when the Dream's key checks out, "Edited" after a change.

content warning

hidden behind a warning until u press Show Dream.

focused

the focus ring on the card itself, like after VoiceOver lands on it.

js

// liking is one press, and the app says the new state out loud (js/core.js, cut down)
likeBtn.addEventListener("click", function () {
  const liked = likeBtn.classList.contains("liked"); // the words carry the state: Like or Unlike (no aria-pressed)
  likeBtn.classList.toggle("liked", !liked);
  likeBtn.querySelector("span").textContent = liked ? "Like" : "Unlike";
  announce(liked ? "Like removed" : "Liked"); // see the live region page
});

it uses the classes from css/app.css and css/a11y.css, so it looks right anywhere in looscid.

how it works w voiceover

  • swipe to the card and VoiceOver reads it all in one go: "Ava. 3 AM thought: what if clouds are just the sky's drafts. 2 hours ago". the dots and the handle are hidden so it doesn't read junk.
  • keep swiping for the buttons: "4 replies, button", "3 Redreams, pop up button", "Like, 12 likes, button", "Share, button", then "More, Dream by Ava, pop up button". after u like it, Like reads "Unlike, 13 likes".
  • every More button says whose Dream it is, so the rotor list isn't just a row of "More".
  • "Verified" and "Edited" are added at the end of the card's label.
  • braille display: the card shows as a short line ("D: Ava 2h: the words") when braille output is on in Settings, Accessibility, Sense. Enter on a button presses it.
