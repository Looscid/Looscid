Components

every piece of looscid, one page each: a live demo of every state, the code to copy and own, and how it works w VoiceOver.

the idea: u don't install anything. find the piece u need, look at its states, copy the code into ur part of looscid and it's yours. the demos are look-only, pressing stuff in them does nothing.

  • Bottom tab bar

    the 5 tabs at the bottom of every screen: Home, Discover, Create, Inbox and Profile.

  • Create dialog

    the sheet that pops up from the Create tab: Dream, Circle, List, New Chat or a chat with Cherry.

  • Dream card

    one Dream in a feed: who made it, the words, and the Reply, Redream, Like and More buttons.

  • Like and ReDream buttons

    the two tick-box buttons under every Dream: Like, and ReDream or Quote.

  • Like and Dislike buttons

    the shared counter: Like and Dislike on Dreams and Cherry replies, and Agree / Disagree on bug reports.

  • Liked manager

    the Liked tab's tools: Likes or Dislikes, a Filter, Unlike / Remove dislike on each line and Clear all shown.

  • More menu list

    the list of big rows on the More screen, plus the Apps group that opens and closes.

  • Pop-up menu

    a button that opens a short menu, like Sort, a Dream's More menu and ReDream.

  • Tabs

    the row of tabs at the top of a screen, like For You, Following and Federated.

  • Checkboxes

    real checkboxes for every on/off setting. never toggles.

  • Radio group

    pick exactly one: a fieldset with a legend and real radio buttons, like Text size.

  • Native select picker

    a plain select with a visible label above it, Fork Lab style. the phone's own picker opens.

  • BackHeader

    the top bar on inner screens: a Back button and the screen's h1.

  • Buttons

    the main button, the outline button and the round icon button.

  • Text field

    a text box with a real label above it, and a short message under it when something's off.

  • Search field and results

    the search box on Discover and the Dreamor rows it finds.

  • Commandbar

    type a command, press Enter, get the answer. the fastest way around looscid w a keyboard or braille display.

  • Circle header

    the top of a Circle: its name, what it's about, member count and the Join button.

  • Profile header

    the top of a profile: name, handle, bio, Edit profile, More and the follow counts.

  • Live region announcements

    how looscid says things out loud, like "Liked" or "2 results". we don't do pop-up toasts.

  • Empty state

    what a list says when there's nothing in it yet.

  • Mod reason picker

    what a mod sees when removing a Dream: pick a reason or type one. the reason is always public.

  • Report a Dream dialog

    report a Dream in 2 taps: pick what's wrong, done. the person never finds out who reported.

  • Report a problem and report rows

    our own issue tracker: the report form and the public list of reports w Agree and Disagree.

  • Skip to content link

    the first link on every screen. jumps past the top bar straight to the content.
