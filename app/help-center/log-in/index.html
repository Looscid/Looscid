Log in on another device, or log out

Add a login method to your LooscidID so you can use it on your other devices. And here's how to log out without losing anything.

Your LooscidID is made on your device, so your other devices don't know about it yet. A login method is how you tell them. See your login methods. Your local profile on this device is always on, so you can't accidentally lock yourself out of your own phone.

Press Add login method, then Choose a network. You can pick Nostr, Mastodon and the fediverse, Bluesky, Funkwhale or Hubzilla. Follow the steps on the screen for the one you picked. For Nostr, you paste your secret key, which starts with nsec1. It stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.

Then, on your other device, open Looscid, press Login, and add the same login method. Boom, same you, two places. Like a hologram, but less creepy.

Being findable, or not

Choose whether people can find you by your login. It's under Discoverability. You can also link an ID, like your npub or your Bluesky handle, so it shows on your LooscidID. That's under Link an ID.

Keep your secret key secret

Never share your secret key, the one that starts with nsec, with anyone. Not your best friend, not a “Looscid mod” in your DMs, not a very convincing cat. Your public key starts with npub1, and that one is totally safe to share.

Logging out

To log out, press Logout in the More menu on your Profile. Looscid asks first, so you can't do it by sneezing on your screen. Heads up: logging out removes your LooscidID and any Nostr key saved on this device. If you haven't saved your key or a backup file somewhere else, it's gone for good. Back up your stuff first. Future you will say thanks.
