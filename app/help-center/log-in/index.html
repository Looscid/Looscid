Log in and log out

Add a login method to your LooscidID so you can log in with it on other devices.

Steps

  1. Press Open LooscidID (Profile, More, Settings).
  2. Press Login methods. Your local profile on this device is always on.
  3. Press Add login method, then Choose a network: Nostr, Mastodon and the fediverse, Bluesky, Funkwhale or Hubzilla.
  4. Follow the steps on the screen for that network. For Nostr, you paste your secret key, which starts with nsec1. It stays on this device and is never sent anywhere.
  5. When you link one, you choose whether people can find you by it.
  6. On another device, open Looscid, press Login, and add the same login method.
  7. To log out, press Profile, then More, then Logout. Looscid asks first, so you can't do it by accident.

Good to know

  • Never share your secret key (nsec) with anyone. Your public key starts with npub1, and that one is safe to share.
