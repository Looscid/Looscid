Send a private chat

Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

Chats are for the stuff that doesn't need to be a Dream. Only you and the other person can read them. They're end-to-end encrypted with Nostr (NIP-17), which is a nerdy way of saying nobody in the middle can peek. Not even me. I tried. Just kidding. I can't.

Chats need a Nostr key on your LooscidID. If you don't have one yet, Log in on another device, or log out shows you how to add one.

Starting a chat

Press Create, then New Chat. Or open Inbox, press Showing Alerts, choose Chats, then press Send a message. In To, type who you want to message, write your message, and send it.

Finding your chats

Your chats are in Inbox, under Chats, in DMs. Requests has chats from people you don't know yet, so strangers don't land right in your main chats. Pinned keeps your favorite chats at the top.

Choose who can message you, if you'd like fewer surprises.
