Start a private chat

Chats are private messages between you and one other Dreamor. Only the two of you can read them.

Steps

  1. Chats need a Nostr key on your LooscidID. If you don't have one yet, see Log in and log out.
  2. Press Create, then New Chat. Or press Inbox, press Showing Alerts, choose Chats, then press Send a message.
  3. In To, type who you want to message, then write your message and send it.
  4. Your chats are in Inbox, Chats, under DMs. Requests has chats from people you don't know yet, and Pinned keeps chats at the top.
  5. Choose who can message you in Open Chats (Profile, More, Settings).

Good to know

  • Chats are end-to-end encrypted with Nostr (NIP-17).
