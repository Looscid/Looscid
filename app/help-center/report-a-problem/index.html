Report a problem

Found a bug or got an idea? Report it right in Looscid. Everyone can read the reports, agree or disagree, and see what the mods say.

Steps

  1. Open Looscid and press Help Center (Profile, More, Help). Open this article, Report a problem. More, Help, Report a problem opens it too.
  2. Pick Bug or Idea, type a short title and say what happened. Looscid adds the app and its version by itself.
  3. Press Send report. It's signed with your Looscid key, so you need one. No other account, no email.
  4. Every report is in the list below it, for everyone to read: its title, Bug or Idea, its status (new, working on it, fixed or closed) and how many people agree and disagree.
  5. Press Agree or Disagree on any report. You get one vote per report, and you can change it or take it back. Nobody sees who voted, only the counts.
  6. Looscid mods set the status, reply as Looscid mods, and close a report with a reason. All of it is public.

Reports

To report a problem, or agree or disagree with one, open it in Looscid: Help Center, Report a problem. It uses your Looscid key.


The report list loads here with JavaScript on. It is also in the app: Help Center, Report a problem.

    Good to know

    • Choose Show above the list to see only new, working on it, fixed or closed reports.
    • Delete my Rem data (Settings, LooscidID) also deletes your reports and your votes.
