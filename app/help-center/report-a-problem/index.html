Report a bug or share an idea

Found a bug or got an idea? Report it right in Looscid. Everyone can read the reports, agree or disagree, and see what the mods say.

Looscid is built by one person, which means bugs happen. Sometimes a lot of them. Sometimes they have families. When you find one, or you have an idea to make Looscid better, tell me right here. In the app, the Report form is in this article. You can also open it from Help in the More menu.

Sending a report

Pick Bug or Idea, type a short title, and say what happened. Looscid adds the app and its version by itself, so you don't have to dig for it. Press Send report. It's signed with your Looscid key, so you need one. No other account, no email.

Everyone can see it

Every report is in the list, for everyone to read: its title, Bug or Idea, its status (new, working on it, fixed or closed), and how many people agree and disagree. Choose Show above the list to see only one status.

Press Agree or Disagree on any report. You get one vote per report, and you can change it or take it back. Nobody sees who voted, only the counts. So yes, you can disagree with your friend's idea and they'll never know.

Looscid mods set the status, reply as Looscid mods, and close a report with a reason. All of it is public, so nothing gets swept under the rug.

Delete my Rem data also deletes your reports and your votes.

Reports

To report a problem, or agree or disagree with one, open it in Looscid: Help Center, Report a problem. It uses your Looscid key.


The report list loads here with JavaScript on. It is also in the app: Help Center, Report a problem.
