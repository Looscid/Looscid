Getting Started

New here? Start with these. Make your LooscidID, log in, and learn your way around.

Articles in this collection

  1. What is Looscid?

    Looscid is a free, open source social app that runs in your web browser, on any phone, tablet or computer.

  2. Make your LooscidID

    Your LooscidID is your account. Looscid makes it on your device the first time you open it.

  3. Log in and log out

    Add a login method to your LooscidID so you can log in with it on other devices.

  4. Get around Looscid

    Where everything is: the tab bar, the top bar, the More menu and the footer.

  5. Use Commandbar

    Commandbar lets you type what you want, like new dream or open settings, and Looscid does it.
