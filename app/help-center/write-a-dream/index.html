Write your first Dream

A Dream can be a few words, a photo, a poll or a link.

Steps

  1. Press Create. It's in the top bar and in the tab bar.
  2. Press Dream. The New Dream screen opens.
  3. Type your Dream in the Dream content box.
  4. If you like, press Add Photo, Add Poll, or Attach for a link or a place.
  5. Press Audience to choose who sees it. Everyone is the default. You can add a Content warning too.
  6. Press Dream. You always press Dream yourself; nothing is sent without you.
  7. Not ready yet? Close it and keep it in Open Drafts (Profile, More).

Good to know

  • Until you add a Nostr key to your LooscidID, your Dreams are saved only on this device. See Log in and log out.
  • Read back my Dream reads it to you before you send it.
