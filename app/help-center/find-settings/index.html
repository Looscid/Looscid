Find any setting

Settings has every choice in Looscid, and a Search box to find one fast.

Steps

  1. Press Open Settings (Profile, More).
  2. Type in Search to find a setting by name, or press a group like Privacy or Accessibility.
  3. Reset is the last item in every Settings list. It always asks first and lets you pick what goes back.
