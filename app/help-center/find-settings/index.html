Find settings and make Looscid yours

Settings has every choice in Looscid, with a Search box to find one fast. Change how Looscid looks, sounds and moves.

Looscid has a lot of settings. Like, a lot. I'm not sorry. Everyone uses their device differently, and Looscid should fit you, not the other way around. Open Settings.

Type in Search to find a setting by name, or press a group like Privacy & Security or Accessibility. Reset is the last item in every Settings list. It always asks first and lets you pick what goes back, so you can't reset everything by accident.

How it looks

Change colors, text and the heading character. The heading character is the little robot around headings and buttons, also called Unspeakable characters. Pick None to turn it off, if the robot isn't your vibe.

Choose your tab bar and the order of profile tabs. Both are on the Tabs screen. Change how media plays and how Dreams get translated, in Media and translation. See Cherry's settings, too. They're in Customization, with Settings Order and Tabs.

How it sounds

Change Looscid's short sounds and their volume. Those are Earcons, little sounds that tell you something happened. Rather see a cue than hear one? Swap sounds for a visual cue. The same switch is in Earcons too.

How it moves

Turn Calm motion on or off. It's one switch for calmer motion, and it starts out matching your device's Reduce Motion setting. Blocking flashing and strobes stays on unless you turn it off yourself. Your eyes and brain deserve that.

Everything else

Change vision, hearing, motion and more. And if you're a keyboard person, change keys and make your own shortcuts.
