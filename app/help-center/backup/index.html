Back up, download or delete your data

Save your settings as one file, back up everything with your key, download your Dreams, and see what's stored on this device.

Phones fall in toilets. Laptops die at the worst moment. Browsers get cleared by little brothers. Backups are how you laugh about it later instead of crying now. Everything on this page lives in one place: Your data.

Back up your settings

On Your data, Back up my settings has Export all settings. Press it, and Looscid saves everything as one file. Keep the file somewhere safe, like your Files app, cloud storage or a USB drive. Not the toilet-phone.

To bring your settings back on any device, open Your data again, press Import settings from a file, then pick the file. Everything's back the way you like it.

A settings backup has your settings, your Cherry chats and the Circles you made. It never has your keys, passwords or Dreams. That's on purpose, so a settings file is safe to keep around.

Back up everything, key included

To save your key too, use Backup file on the same screen. It puts everything and your key in one locked file. This is the one that saves you if you lose your device or log out, so make one. Seriously. I'll wait.

Download your stuff

Your data also has Export my data and Download my Dreams. Each one has a line saying exactly what it saves, so no guessing.

What's on this device

See what's stored on this device. Delete local data is on the same screen too. Your Dreams on Rem are a different story, and About Rem tells it.

While you're at it, check how safe your LooscidID is. Security checkup suggests ways to keep it safe.
