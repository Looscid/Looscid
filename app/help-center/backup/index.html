Back up and bring back your settings

Save every setting as one file, and bring it back on any device.

Steps

  1. Press Open Backup (Profile, More, Settings, General).
  2. Press Export all settings. Looscid saves one file.
  3. Keep the file somewhere safe, like your Files app, cloud storage or a USB drive.
  4. On any device, open Backup again and press Import settings from a file, then pick the file.

Good to know

  • A backup has your settings, Cherry chats and the Circles you made. It never has your keys, passwords or Dreams.
