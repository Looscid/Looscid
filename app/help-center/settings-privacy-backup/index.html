Settings, privacy and your data

Find any setting, choose who can reach you, learn about Rem, and back up your stuff.

Articles in this collection

  1. Find settings and make Looscid yours

    Settings has every choice in Looscid, with a Search box to find one fast. Change how Looscid looks, sounds and moves.

  2. Privacy and security

    Choose who can see and reach you, and keep your LooscidID safe. Dreams set to Everyone are public on Rem; your drafts and settings stay on your device.

  3. About Rem: where your Dreams live

    Rem is Looscid's own server. It's where cooked Dreams live, so your Dreams show up on all your devices and other Dreamors can find them. Here's everything it does, everything it doesn't, and what it might have to do with sleep.

  4. Back up, download or delete your data

    Save your settings as one file, back up everything with your key, download your Dreams, and see what's stored on this device.
