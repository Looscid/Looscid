Follow a thread

Follow a thread to get an alert when someone replies.

Steps

  1. Open the thread by pressing Replies under a Dream.
  2. Press Follow Thread. It's just above the tab bar.
  3. New replies now show up in your Inbox, under Followed Threads.
  4. Your Profile has a Followed Threads tab with every thread you follow.
  5. To stop, press Unfollow Thread in the same place.
  6. Choose who can see the threads you follow in Open Privacy (Profile, More, Settings), Followed Threads.
