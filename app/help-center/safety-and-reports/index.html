Reports, safety and the Modlog

Report a Dream, a person or a Circle, report a bug or an idea, and see why Dreams get removed.

Articles in this collection

  1. Report a Dream, a person or a Circle

    Report a Dream, a person, a Circle or child safety, then follow what happens and talk to the mods. Reports are private.

  2. Report a bug or share an idea

    Found a bug or got an idea? Report it right in Looscid. Everyone can read the reports, agree or disagree, and see what the mods say.

  3. The Modlog: why Dreams get removed

    Every Dream a Circle's mods or a Looscid admin removed is listed in public, with the date and the reason.
