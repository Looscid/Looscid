Dream Options and Dreamor Options

The More button on a Dream, and on someone's profile, holds everything else you can do.

Steps

  1. Press More under a Dream. Dream Options opens, with things like Copy link and Pin to profile for your own Dreams.
  2. On your own Dream or reply, Dream Options also has Edit and Delete.
  3. On someone's profile, press More. It has Share profile, Copy link, Add to list, Mute and Block.
  4. Press Close, or Escape on a keyboard, to close a menu without choosing.
