Your Inbox and alerts

Inbox has your alerts, like replies, mentions and likes, and your private chats.

Steps

  1. Press Open Inbox (tab bar).
  2. Showing Alerts switches between Alerts and Chats.
  3. Press All, Unread, Replies, Mentions, Likes and the rest to see one kind of alert.
  4. Press Mark all read when you're caught up.
  5. Each alert has Alert Options.
  6. Choose which alerts you get in Open Alerts (Profile, More, Settings).
