Join or make a Circle

A Circle is a group of Dreamors around one thing you like.

Steps

  1. Press Open Circles (Profile, More).
  2. Press Discover to find Circles to join, or My Circles for the ones you're in.
  3. To make your own, press Create, then Circle, and follow the steps.
  4. If you run a Circle, each member has Member Options for them.
