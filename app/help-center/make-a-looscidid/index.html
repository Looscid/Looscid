Make your LooscidID

Your LooscidID is your account. Looscid makes it on your device the first time you open it, so there's no sign-up form and no email.

Most apps make you fill out a form, pick a password, confirm your email, prove you're not a robot, and then confirm your email again for some reason. Looscid skips all of that. The first time you open looscid.github.io/Looscid, the welcome screens start by themselves.

Read the welcome, then press Yes, I'm ready. If you already know what you're doing, press Skip intro. I won't be offended. Much.

Next, Setting up Looscid asks how you use your device. Pick any choices that fit you, or none at all, then press Set up Looscid. You can change all of it later, so don't stress about getting it perfect.

You exist now

That's it. Your LooscidID lives on this device, and it starts out with the name Dreamor and the username @dreamor. That's a great name, but it's also everyone's name at first, so you'll probably want your own. Change your name and username in your LooscidID, under Profile. You can also press Edit profile on your Profile tab.

To put your Dreams on Rem, your LooscidID needs a key. Until you make one, your cooked Dreams wait patiently on your device, and they go on Rem the moment you make it. No Dream gets left behind.

Using it on more than one device

Your LooscidID starts out on one device. To use it on another phone or computer too, add a login method. Log in on another device, or log out walks you through it.

Logged out by accident and now you're in Guest Mode? It happens. Press Create a LooscidID to make one again.
