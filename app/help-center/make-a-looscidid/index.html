Make your LooscidID

Your LooscidID is your account. It's a secret key Looscid makes on your device in one step, so there's no sign-up form and no email.

Most apps make you fill out a form, pick a password, confirm your email, prove you're not a robot, and then confirm your email again for some reason. Looscid skips all of that. The first time you open looscid.github.io/Looscid, the welcome screens start by themselves.

Read the welcome, then press Yes, I'm ready. If you already know what you're doing, press Skip intro. I won't be offended. Much.

Next, Setting up Looscid asks how you use your device. Pick any choices that fit you, or none at all, then press Set up Looscid. You can change all of it later, so don't stress about getting it perfect.

Make your LooscidID

After setup, you're a Guest. Guests can look around everything: the feed, Discover, profiles and Circles. To Dream, reply, like or follow, you need a LooscidID. Make your LooscidID and press Create a LooscidID. The same button shows up the first time you try to Dream, like or follow as a Guest, so you never have to go looking for it.

Pick a passcode, type it again, and press Create my LooscidID. Four short words work well, like blue lamp river jump. Looscid makes your key right there on your device and locks it with your passcode. That's the moment you become a Dreamor. Welcome. We have snacks. (We don't have snacks.)

Save your key

Next, Looscid shows your secret key one time. Save it somewhere safe: your Passwords app, a note, or your BrailleNote. Then press I saved my key. If you ever lose this device, your key is the only way back in. Nobody can reset it, not even me, and that's what keeps hackers out too.

Your name

Your LooscidID starts out with the name Dreamor and the username @dreamor. That's a great name, but it's also everyone's name at first, so you'll probably want your own. Change your name and username in your LooscidID, under Profile. You can also press Edit profile on your Profile tab.

Made a LooscidID before November 9, 2026, back when it came without a key? Your LooscidID screen says Your LooscidID needs a key, with a Make my key button. One step, and your Dreams start going to Rem.

Using it on more than one device

Your LooscidID starts out on one device. To use it on another phone or computer too, add a login method. Log in on another device, or log out walks you through it.

Logged out by accident and now you're in Guest Mode? It happens. Press Create a LooscidID to make a new one, or bring yours back with your key or a backup file.
