Make your LooscidID

Your LooscidID is your account. Looscid makes it on your device the first time you open it.

Steps

  1. Open looscid.github.io/Looscid. The welcome screens start by themselves the first time.
  2. Read the welcome, then press Yes, I'm ready. Or press Skip intro.
  3. Setting up Looscid asks how you use your device. Pick any choices that fit you, or none, then press Set up Looscid. You can change them later.
  4. That's it: your LooscidID now exists on this device. It starts with the name Dreamor and the username @dreamor.
  5. To pick your own name and username, press Open LooscidID (Profile, More, Settings), then Profile. You can also press Edit profile on your Profile tab.
  6. To use your LooscidID on another device too, add a login method. See Log in and log out.

Good to know

  • Logged out? You're in Guest Mode. Press Create a LooscidID to make one again.
