What is Looscid?

Looscid is a free, open source social app that runs in your web browser, on any phone, tablet or computer.

Steps

  1. Open looscid.github.io/Looscid in any web browser. There is nothing to install and nothing to pay.
  2. A Dream is what you share on Looscid: a few words, a photo, a poll or a link. People on Looscid are Dreamors.
  3. Home shows Dreams. Discover helps you find Dreamors and Circles. Inbox has your alerts and private chats. Profile is you.
  4. Circles are groups of Dreamors around one thing you like. Cherry is the assistant built into Looscid.
  5. Everything stays on your device until you choose to send or share it. Looscid has no ads and doesn't sell your data.

Good to know

  • In this Help Center, press means tap on a touch screen, click with a mouse, or Enter or Space on a keyboard.
