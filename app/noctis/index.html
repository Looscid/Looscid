Introducing Noctis, formerly Cherry

Published Thursday, November 19, 2026

Why we renamed Cherry

Looscid's on-device assistant has been called Cherry. It is now called Noctis. The new name is shorter to say and easier to tell apart from other products that share the name Cherry.

The name change does not change how the assistant works. It still runs on your device, and your words are not sent to an AI company.

What Noctis does

Noctis is the assistant built into Looscid. It is off until you turn it on. The first time you turn it on, it downloads its model once. After that, it works offline.

  • Helps you write Dreams with accessibility in mind, including checks for alt text, heading structure, and sentence length.
  • Recommends relays based on reliability and location, and explains the trade-offs.
  • Suggests Circle guidelines and content policies.
  • Checks that a Dream does not leak personal information before you publish it.
  • Explains Nostr protocol features and relay specifications in plain language.

Our core values

Accessibility first
Noctis is built for VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, and Braille displays. Accessibility is a requirement, not an extra feature.
Privacy
Noctis does not collect, store, or expose personal data.
Nostr
Your identity is your public key. Your Dreams are signed events that you can publish to relays of your choice.
Decentralized
No single company controls the network or your identity.
Open source
Code and models stay open so anyone can inspect and improve them.

What is changing and what is not

Cherry is now Noctis in the assistant's name and its descriptions. Some web addresses and file names still use the old name for now so existing links keep working.

Tell us what you think

If something about the new name or the assistant is hard to use with your assistive technology, please tell us. Accessibility reports are the most useful feedback we can get.
