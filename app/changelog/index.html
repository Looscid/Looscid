Looscid roadmap and changelog

out now

live today.

  • looscid in ur browser: Dreams and Replies, Discover, Alerts, Soundpacks and settings backup.
  • LooscidID: ur account, made right on ur device. add a login method, like Nostr, Mastodon or Bluesky, to use it on ur other devices.
  • the Help Center, with step-by-step guides.
  • the Looscid Discord, with its own channels for 13 languages.
  • faith channels on the Discord: pick what u celebrate (optional), and u get a private channel with Dreamors who share ur language and faith.
  • holiday messages, starting November 8: LooscidBOT wishes each language and faith happy holidays on its own holidays, in its own language, no pings.

up next

circles + the discord

  • LooscidID profiles: sign up, log in, and add ur language and what u celebrate. both are optional.
  • we're looking into it: ur Nostr key as ur LooscidID, so there's no password to remember or leak.
  • connect LooscidID to Discord: Linked Roles sync ur language and faith roles automatically. change em in looscid once, and Discord follows.
  • Looscid Circle as a Discord requirement: a server can ask for a Looscid Circle in its Linked Roles settings.
  • import from Discord: a button in Settings that takes ur Discord data download and brings it into looscid.
  • server copy: LooscidBOT saves a server's channels, roles, permissions and join questions, so u can import em into a Looscid Circle.
  • friend suggestions: u can always add a friend urself, and that never depends on anyone else. on top of that, if u and another Dreamor were in the same server, we can suggest u add each other. it's opt-in, both of u need to link ur LooscidID, and it only ever suggests. we never add anyone for u.

pins, feeds + the more menu

  • pins: pin anything, like Dreams, Dreamors, hashtags, apps, Cherry chats and actions. pin from any item's options button, with a long press, or from the Commandbar.
  • u pick where each pin goes: as a feed tab (Dreamors and hashtags), in a quick menu (one menu button with ur top pins and "See all pins"), or just saved.
  • move or unpin a feed tab from that tab's More menu, or from ur feed settings.
  • pinned Dreams work like bookmarks. group pins into folders. Cherry can suggest pins, only if u turn that on.
  • Commandbar commands for pins, like "pins", "pin @name" and "unpin #tag", and for moving feed tabs.
  • ur pins are private. pinned chats always stay private. if u want, show a public pin list on ur profile, and pick what's on it.
  • no separate Apps section: just pin an app for quick access.
  • a Feeds menu that holds ur feeds, Circles and places in one spot, tied into the quick menu.
  • Create always stays the middle tab.
  • the classic More menu is back: ur profile card on top and a short line under each item explaining it, with everything new added in.
  • settings styles: pick how Settings is laid out, same settings in every style. Classic is one long list. Sections groups em with headings and a search box on top, and it comes first. Sidebar shows Sections on wide screens. the style u pick never changes unless u change it.
  • a fresh new look for Cherry, designed by 2three1y.
  • new features live where u already are, not on new pages.

who sees ur Dream

  • pick who sees each Dream: Public, Dreamors you follow, My people, or Only me.
  • set a default audience in Settings, so every new Dream starts with it. change it on any Dream, anytime.
  • My people: ur own close list (the name isn't final). nobody gets told when u add or remove them. when u add ppl, ur mutuals show up first, with tabs for Mutuals, Following and Followers.
  • private mode: everything u make stays just with u til u turn it off.
  • privacy checkup in Settings → Privacy: a quick walk through ur privacy settings, one step at a time.
  • the audience picker reads clean with VoiceOver: each choice is one swipe, with its name and a one-line explanation.

looscid apps (real ones)

  • android app is coming first, built in kotlin, open source (AGPL), in its own repo, Looscid/Looscid-Android. it opens looscid first, then we make screens native one by one.
  • ios app comes after, in swift, works all the way back to ios 15 (where looscid started), in its own repo, Looscid/Looscid-iOS. u install it by sideloading.
  • on ios 15, the app tells u once which features don't work there, and a Help Center page lists em.
  • updates: looscid updates itself when u open it, and most updates land instantly. when the app itself needs an update, a "Looscid needs an update" alert pops up. tap it n ur done.
  • an update screen with what's new that u can open whenever. it updates the apps inside looscid too.
  • get looscid from open-source android app stores.
  • the apps page links every app to where u can get it.
  • braille displays: Enter submits one-line fields. in the message box, Enter sends and Shift+Enter makes a new line, and a Settings checkbox, "Enter sends messages", controls it. Enter also hits buttons, opens menus, checks boxes, and runs the swipe action ur on, like Like Dream. all of it works on any braille display, not just the fancy ones.
  • works on privacy-focused android phones with no Google services.
  • ur looscid key stays locked up safe in ur phone's secure storage.
  • real notifications for Alerts.
  • share to looscid from other apps.
  • a home screen shortcut to start a Dream.
  • read stuff out loud with any voice that's already on ur phone.
  • read-aloud built in: the app can read Dreams out loud with its own voice. it's a Settings checkbox, off by default, so it never talks over ur screen reader.
  • a "Read Dream aloud" swipe action on every Dream, so if u use a screen reader u can hear a Dream in one swipe.
  • a voice shop inside looscid where u download as many voices as u want. voices from other makers are labeled with who made em. using these voices across ur whole phone needs ios 16+, so that one goes on the "doesn't work on ios 15" list.
  • our own offline voices, open n shareable.

safety + privacy

  • "Friends of Friends": a new choice for who can see ur Dream.
  • mods give a reason when they take down a Dream.
  • blocklists.

likes, dislikes + labels

  • sort ur likes + dislikes (newest, oldest, by kind).
  • a spot that shows public bug report votes by name (rn looscid only shows the counts).
  • when Cherry makes a Dream, it gets an automatic "AI generated" label that can't be removed. people can add their own content labels too (like AI-made, sensitive or spoilers), and we'll add more over time.
  • Calm mode (a Settings checkbox, off by default): Dreams w flashing lights get swapped for a placeholder w the description, even from people u follow. for flashing lights there's no "show anyway" button, period.
  • Calm mode also puts videos w no sound or no description behind a placeholder, w a "show anyway" button.
  • flash check: when a video is uploaded, looscid counts how fast the brightness flips (the 3-flashes-a-second rule) and adds the flashing lights label on its own. just math, no AI.
  • upload nudge: before u Dream a video or image, we ask "any flashing lights?" and "add a description".
  • anyone can suggest a label or write a description for a Dream. once enough people agree it sticks, and mods can add em too.

someday

  • moving everyone from the Looscid Discord into looscid itself, with Circles for every language and faith. LooscidBOT's holiday code comes with us.
