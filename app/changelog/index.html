Now

Live today.

  • Looscid in your browser: Dreams and Replies, Discover, Alerts, Soundpacks and settings backup.
  • LooscidID: your account, made on your device. Add a login method, like Nostr, Mastodon or Bluesky, to use it on other devices.
  • The Help Center, with step-by-step guides.
  • The Looscid Discord, with its own channels for 13 languages.
  • Faith channels on the Discord: pick what you celebrate (optional), and you get a private channel with Dreamors who share your language and faith.
  • Holiday messages, starting November 8: LooscidBOT wishes each language and faith happy holidays on its own holidays, in its own language, with no pings.

Next

Circles and Discord

  • LooscidID profiles: sign up, log in, and add your language and what you celebrate. Both are optional.
  • Exploring: your Nostr key as your LooscidID, so there's no password to remember or leak.
  • Connect LooscidID to Discord: Linked Roles sync your language and faith roles automatically. Change them in Looscid once, and Discord follows.
  • Looscid Circle as a Discord requirement: a server can ask for a Looscid Circle in its Linked Roles settings.
  • Import from Discord: a button in Settings that takes your Discord data download and brings it into Looscid.
  • Server copy: LooscidBOT saves a server's channels, roles, permissions and join questions, so you can import them into a Looscid Circle.
  • Friend suggestions: you can always add a friend yourself, and that never depends on anyone else. On top of that, if you and another Dreamor were in the same server, Looscid can suggest you add each other. It's opt-in, both of you need to link your LooscidID, and it only ever suggests. It never adds anyone for you.

Pins, feeds and the More menu

  • Pins: pin anything, like Dreams, Dreamors, hashtags, apps, Cherry chats and actions. Pin from any item's options button, with a long press, or from the Commandbar.
  • You choose where each pin goes: as a feed tab (Dreamors and hashtags), in a quick menu (one menu button with your top pins and "See all pins"), or just saved.
  • Move or unpin a feed tab from that tab's More menu, or from your feed settings.
  • Pinned Dreams work like bookmarks. Group pins into folders. Cherry can suggest pins, only if you turn that on.
  • Commandbar commands for pins, like "pins", "pin @name" and "unpin #tag", and for moving feed tabs.
  • Your pins are private. Pinned chats always stay private. If you want, show a public pin list on your profile, and choose what's on it.
  • No separate Apps section: pin an app for quick access.
  • A Feeds menu that holds your feeds, Circles and places in one spot, tied into the quick menu.
  • Create always stays the middle tab.
  • The classic More menu is back: your profile card on top and a short description under each item, with everything new added in.
  • Settings styles: pick how Settings is laid out, with the same settings in every style. Classic is one long list. Sections groups them with headings and a search box on top, and comes first. Sidebar shows Sections on wide screens. The style you pick never changes unless you change it.
  • A new look for Cherry, designed by 2three1y.
  • New features live where you already are, not on new pages.

Who sees your Dream

  • Pick who sees each Dream: Public, Dreamors you follow, My people, or Only me.
  • Set a default audience in Settings, so every new Dream starts with it. Change it on any Dream, anytime.
  • My people: your own close list (the name isn't final). People aren't told when you add or remove them. When you add people, your mutuals are suggested at the top, with tabs for Mutuals, Following and Followers.
  • Private mode: everything you make stays only with you until you turn it off.
  • Privacy checkup: a short walk through your privacy settings, one step at a time.
  • The audience picker reads clearly with VoiceOver: each choice is one swipe, with its name and a one-line description.

Safety and privacy

  • "Friends of Friends": a new choice for who can see your Dream.
  • Moderators give a reason when they remove a Dream.
  • Blocklists.

Later

  • Moving everyone from the Looscid Discord into Looscid itself, with Circles for every language and faith. LooscidBOT's holiday code comes along.
