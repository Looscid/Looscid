# Rules

## 1. Be Kind

Treat all Dreamors with respect. Harassment, bullying or targeted hate speech is not permitted on Looscid.

## 2. Authentic Dreams

Post authentic content. Do not impersonate others, spread misinformation or use automated accounts without disclosure.

## 3. Safe Content

Do not post violent, sexually explicit or otherwise harmful content. Content involving minors in any inappropriate context is strictly forbidden.

## 4. Groups

Groups must have a clear, positive purpose. Group moderators must enforce these guidelines within their communities.

## 5. Reporting

Use the report button on any Dream, reply or profile to flag content that violates these guidelines. Our team reviews all reports.

## 6. Consequences

Violations may result in content removal, account suspension or permanent bans depending on severity. Last updated: February 2026.
