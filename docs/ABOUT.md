# About Looscid

## In short {#in-short}

Looscid is a free social app where you cook Dreams. It works with every screen reader, and nothing in it flashes more than three times a second.

No ads. I don't sell your data. And when you report something, a real person reads it.

## What you do here {#what-you-do-here}

You write a Dream, cook it (that's our word for sending it), and it lands in people's feeds. Reply, Like, ReDream the ones you love, and join Circles with people into the same stuff. People here are called Dreamors.

There's no sign-up form. Looscid makes your LooscidID on your device the first time you open it. No email, no phone number.

## Why I'm building this {#why-i-m-building-this-carter}

I'm Carter, and I'm blind. I got tired of apps that treat accessibility like a sticker on the box, so I'm building the one I wanted.

My rule is simple: if something is hard to use with a screen reader, I don't ship it.

## Who it's for {#the-conditions-looscid-is-built-for}

Everyone. But I build first for the people apps forget: blind and low vision, Deaf and hard of hearing, people with motor disabilities, cognitive and learning disabilities, seizures, or motion sickness from screens.

You don't need a disability to show up. You just need to not be weird about people who do.

## Built for every screen reader {#every-screen-reader}

VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS, Orca and braille displays all get the same love. Not just the one I use.

Every screen has one main heading. Buttons say what they do, like "Delete Dream", not just "Delete". Focus only moves when you move it. Nothing yanks you around.

## Cherry and Rem {#cherry-and-rem}

Cherry is the AI helper built into Looscid, and it runs on your device. It's there when you want it, not glued to every button.

Rem is Looscid's own server. It holds cooked Dreams set to Everyone, so they show up on all your devices and other Dreamors can find them. REM is also the part of sleep where you dream. Coincidence? I'll let you decide. [About Rem](https://looscid.github.io/Looscid/help/rem/) has the long version.

## Your stuff and your privacy {#privacy}

Drafts stay on your device. So do Dreams set to Followers only, My Circles, or Only this device. Every Dream on Rem is signed with your LooscidID key, so nobody can fake one from you. Rem never sees your secret key.

Want your stuff off Rem? Press Delete my Rem data in [Rem settings](https://looscid.github.io/Looscid/More/Settings/Rem/) and it's gone. Back up your key, though. If you lose it, I can't magic your account back.

## Safety, reports and the Modlog {#report-a-problem-or-appeal-a-ban}

{#the-report-center}
{#report-an-accessibility-bug}
Report a Dream, a person, a Circle, a bug, or a child safety problem in the [Report Center](https://looscid.github.io/Looscid/Report/). Reports about people and Dreams are private. You get a real answer, and My reports shows what happened to each one.

Found an accessibility bug? Report it there too. Tell me your screen reader and device, what you did, and what you heard.

### The Modlog {#the-mod-log}

When the mods remove a Dream, it goes in the public Modlog with the date and the reason. No reason, no removal. There's a public Modlog so removals aren't a secret club.

### If you get banned {#if-you-get-banned}

A ban isn't a gag order. You get to make your case, and I'll listen. There's no appeal button yet, so use [Feedback](https://looscid.github.io/Looscid/Feedback/) for now. It opens a public issue on GitHub, so keep private stuff out of it.

## What makes Looscid different {#what-makes-looscid-different}

{#no-ads}

It's open source under the GNU AGPL, so anyone can read the code and check I'm not up to anything weird. No ads, and no selling your data.

There's no dislike button booing you. If something isn't for you, pick Not interested in the Dream's More menu. You'll see less like it, and nobody else ever knows.

## What I promise you {#my-commitment}

I don't make a screen and fix it for VoiceOver later. If it doesn't work with a screen reader, it isn't done.

If an update breaks something for you, that's a bug, and I fix it.

## What's not done yet {#known-gaps}

I'd rather be honest than act like it's perfect lol.

- Zero animation mode, one switch that stops all motion.
- Flash blocking that also catches strobes inside Dreams.
- A real appeal button for bans.
- A check by an outside accessibility expert. That hasn't happened yet.

If something breaks, tell me. I'd rather fix it than write a long excuse.

## Who's behind it {#team}

A blind dev, mostly solo, with help from testers and the Looscid mods. Some of the best ideas came from people who use it, like Zero animation mode, which came from the epilepsy community. Everyone who helps is on the [Credits](https://looscid.github.io/Looscid/Credits/) page.
